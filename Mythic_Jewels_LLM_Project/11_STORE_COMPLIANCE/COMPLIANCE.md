# STORE & LEGAL COMPLIANCE

## Product boundary
Bu ürün gerçek para ile bahis/kumar uygulaması değildir.

YASAK:
- Real-money betting
- Cash-out
- Real-world prize redemption
- Player-to-player money transfer
- Crypto wagering
- Gambling odds presented as a way to win money

## Virtual economy
Gold, Gems, Energy, Boosters and XP have utility only inside the game.

## Random rewards
Randomized virtual rewards must be transparently described where required by platform policy and applicable law. Drop rates should be configuration-driven and auditable.

## IAP
Only legitimate digital game goods. Purchase restoration and platform-specific billing requirements must be implemented.

## Advertising
Ads should be age-appropriate and integrated through compliant SDKs. Rewarded ads grant predefined in-game rewards only.

## Privacy
Collect minimum necessary data. Privacy policy, consent and regional requirements must be reviewed before release.

## Age rating
Complete current Google Play and Apple age-rating questionnaires truthfully based on the final build.

## IMPORTANT
Before store submission, check the then-current official Google Play and Apple App Store policies. This document is a product-design guardrail, not legal advice.
